DEFLECT

Privacy Policy

DEFLECT does not ask for your name, your email, or an account. It does not know who you are, and it never sends your game data anywhere.

What stays on your device

Everything the game remembers is stored locally, in your browser's or your phone's own storage:

  • Your progress — shards blocked, time survived, longest streak
  • Your ten best runs, with their scores and dates
  • Your chosen theme and sound setting

None of it is uploaded, shared, or readable by us. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently, and there is no copy anywhere else to restore it from.

Advertising

The Android version shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve them, AdMob may access your device's advertising ID and standard technical information such as device type, operating system version, and approximate region.

We do not receive, store, or have any access to this data — it goes to Google, not to us. Google's handling of it is described in the Google Privacy & Terms page.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time from your Android settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

The web version of the game, hosted on itch.io and GitHub Pages, shows no ads and loads no advertising code at all.

Analytics

There are none. The game contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no tracking pixels. We do not know how many times you played or how far you got.

Children

DEFLECT is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to appeal primarily to them. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Permissions

The Android app requests one permission: internet access, used only to load ads. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, files, or any other part of your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date below. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
